# Owner portal redesign

Hallmark 2.0.0, full redesign, app scope. Coral was dealt first of three choices. The system keeps the ForkFlow mark and uses warm neutral surfaces, ink actions, restrained red selections, and ruled records.

## Verification

- Hallmark render sweep: 24 source files and three routes, eight viewport widths. Before: 117 Floor findings and 73 Reflex findings. After: zero Floor, Reflex or unresolved warnings.
- Kept inventory: 56/56 links, 212/212 handlers, 9/9 figures. All real fields remain; the scanner classified two removed decorative Icon `name` props (`calendar`, `check`) as fields. The generic welcome heading became Today.
- Fresh-context finish review: one finding, resolved. Permanent underlines identify the linked totals; the old arrows had made those shortcuts easier to discover.
- Portal typecheck, production build, licensing Node build and 127 portal/licensing-worker tests passed.
- Browser checks: 168 populated and 98 empty viewport/page checks across all five demo accounts, at 1440px and 390px; no page errors or document overflow.
- Expanded module controls, extra invoice line, unsaved-order guard, customer/staff dialogs, drawer Escape and resize recovery passed.

The local comparison is `.hallmark/compare.html` with screenshots in `before/` and `shots/`; these generated files remain in the attached worktree and are gitignored. The populated preview is http://127.0.0.1:5174/portal/home while the local preview processes run.

## Preserved and changed

No routes, data calls, role guards or application components were deleted. No backend or app dependencies changed. Existing loading, empty, error and confirmation behavior remains. Tables, forms and record details now share the portal's single stylesheet; `apps/portal/design.md` records the system.

Copy edits: the dynamic “Welcome back, [name].” heading became “Today”; the generic overview subtitle became the India date and “[name], here is your work for today.” The missing renewal-date dash became “Not set”; the customer range dash became “to”. Middle-dot metadata separators became commas, sentences, parentheses or separate spans. Other operational words and all figures remain as supplied by the app.

No image or content slots require replacement. The sandbox data is for review only. Production deployment remains with the owner.
