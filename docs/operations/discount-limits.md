# Discount limits

Each role has the largest discount it can give on a bill, as a percent of the bill subtotal.
A discount above the limit needs an admin's PIN. Admins have no limit.

Starting limits: cashier 10%, captain/waiter 0%, kitchen 0%. Available on Basic and Pro.

## Set the limits (admin)

1. Open **Users & captains**.
2. Under **Discount limits**, enter a percent for each role and choose **Save discount limits**.
   Leave a role blank for no limit.
3. To give one person a different limit, enter it in the **Discount limit** column of the staff list.
   Clear the box to return that person to their role's limit.

A person's own limit always wins over their role's limit, whether it is higher or lower.

## Giving a larger discount

1. In billing, open **Discount**, enter the amount and the reason.
2. If the amount is above your limit, enter an admin's PIN in **Admin PIN** and review the bill preview.
3. Issue the bill. The approving admin is saved on the bill.

The PIN is checked once and is not stored. Too many wrong PINs pause approvals for a short time,
the same as for voids and refunds. If the PIN field is left empty, the preview shows your limit.

An admin billing directly needs no PIN.
