# Changing an item after it reaches the kitchen

Use **Edit** next to a sent item on the order screen to change its cooking note or reduce its
quantity. Available on Basic and Pro.

## Who can do what

- **Captains/waiters, cashiers and admins** can change the cooking note.
- **Cashiers and admins** can also reduce the quantity, the same people who can cancel a sent item.

To serve **more** of a dish, add it again and send it to the kitchen. It goes on a new ticket.

## Steps

1. Open the order and choose **Edit** next to the sent item.
2. Cashiers and admins: enter the new quantity (from 1 up to the current quantity).
3. Enter the cooking note, or leave it blank for none.
4. If you reduced the quantity, enter a reason.
5. If the restaurant asks each time, choose whether to print a **CHANGED** slip to the kitchen.

To remove the dish completely, use **×** (cancel) instead.

## What happens

- The kitchen slip shows the KOT number, table or parcel, the old and new quantity, the old and
  new note, and the reason.
- The removed units appear on the order as a separate cancelled line with your reason. Bills
  charge only what is left, and the cancellations report lists the removed units.
- Stock used by the removed units is returned. A later cancel of the rest returns only what is
  still on the order, so stock is never returned twice.
- Every change is kept in an edit history that cannot be altered.

## Setting (admin)

**Settings → Kitchen slip when a sent item is changed**:

- **Ask each time** (default): staff choose when they make the change.
- **Always print**.
- **Never print**: the kitchen screen still updates.

The slip prints on the printer of the ticket's kitchen station. If it cannot be printed, the
change is still saved and the screen says the slip was not printed.
