# Tables & orders

The tables screen uses a compact workspace with a fixed header and filters.
Table cards show availability and the number of active orders. Use the status
buttons, search, and **Table area** selector to narrow the view.

On desktop, **Open takeaways** appears beside the tables. Long table and takeaway
lists scroll independently. On phones, **Tables** and **Takeaways** switch between
the lists. A single **Takeaway** button opens the takeaway workflow, with checkout
available to administrators and cashiers. Use **Hold · F8** in a takeaway to keep
it in the open list and make room for the next customer.

Opening a free table starts its order. A table with one active order opens it
directly. A table with multiple bill groups opens a split selection dialog;
choose a group or **New split**. To change a party's table or bill two tables
together, see [moving and merging tables](table-move-merge.md); a table linked
to another table's bill shows **with T3** and opens the combined order.

For table orders, send kitchen items with **KOT**. The table can be billed as soon
as its kitchen items are sent; billing never waits for the kitchen. Items with a
kitchen station must be sent before billing. If the restaurant uses the Kitchen
Display (a Pro add-on turned on in the [Marketplace](marketplace.md)), the kitchen
sees each ticket and clears it with **Done**.

**Reservations** opens the booking schedule. Admins and cashiers manage bookings;
waiters can view them. A currently held table shows **Reserved**, and clicking
it opens its bookings. Use **Seat & open bill** to start the party's order.
See [table reservations](table-reservations.md) for timing and availability rules.

**Manage tables** and **Table QR codes** open dialogs for administrators.
Management retains adding, renaming, ordering, and activating/deactivating
tables. QR management retains enabling, replacing, copying, and printing codes.

The QR and table-service summaries show pending request counts, including while
their dialogs are closed. **Review requests** opens QR orders for staff review.
**View service requests** opens waiter calls and bill requests. Viewing recent
history does not change the pending service count. **Needs attention** indicates
a refresh error; open the dialog for details and refresh controls.

QR acceptance still adds pending order items for the normal kitchen-send flow.
Marking a service request handled does not create a bill or record payment.
Saving actions temporarily block closing dialogs and leaving the screen.
