# Offers and BOGO (backend) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Offers that an admin sets up once (buy X get Y, item discounts, bill discounts above a minimum) apply
automatically to matching bills, with each offer's money on the lines it came from. Backend only; the web UI
goes to Codex (Task 7 writes the handoff).

**Architecture:** A pure domain module (`offers.ts`) decides eligibility and works out per-line offer
discounts. `calculateBill` takes a per-line `offerDiscountPaise` before grouping by GST rate. Migration 63 stores
offers and, per bill, what each offer gave and on which line, and `saveReportLines` puts that on the lines.
The server loads offers in `priceOrder`, applies them before the slab, points and manual discounts, and prints
them on the receipt.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Fastify, better-sqlite3, zod, vitest (npm workspaces).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-offers-design.md`

## Review Focus

1. **One line split across two offers.** 3 Margheritas, "BOGO" 1+1 and "10% off pizzas": BOGO uses 2 units, the
   third gets 10%; the line's discount is the sum. Test in Task 1.
2. **Cross-midnight window at a date edge.** A Fri 22:00–02:00 offer valid until a Friday still applies at 01:00 on
   the Saturday after; it doesn't apply at 01:00 on that Friday. Test in Task 1.
3. **Offers plus a manual discount bigger than what's left.** 400 "The discounts are more than the bill", not a
   negative total. Test in Task 2 (domain) and Task 5 (server).
4. **An offer edited or switched off between preview and issue.** The bill is refused with the existing preview
   mismatch error; nothing is charged unseen. Test in Task 5.
5. **Full refund of a bill with a bill-level offer.** The refund credits exactly the bill total (no paisa lost to the
   bill offer's spread). Test in Task 5.
