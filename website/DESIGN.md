## Direction A: The masala dabba (taste: `one-motif`) — recommended
**Idea:** the round masala dabba, the steel spice tin with its small bowls, becomes the product map. Seven bowls = seven things the software handles: billing, tables and KOT, the Captain app, kitchen display, inventory, reports, online food aggregator orders.
**Why only MasalaPOS can use it:** the name is the idea. A competitor named after something else cannot use a masala tin without it being decoration.
**Hero:** a huge orange circle, cropped by the viewport, holding the real billing screenshot. Short headline, one supporting sentence, one demo button.
**The motif returns as:** the mask for every screenshot, the shape of the primary button, and one full-width orange band between halves of the page.
**Live thing:** tap a bowl to open that module's screenshot and three lines on what it does. Works with a thumb, and falls back to a plain list without script.
**Risk:** the dabba must read as a tin and not a pie chart. Needs one careful drawing.

## Direction B: The bill that builds (taste: `split-stage`)
**Idea:** the bill is the artefact every owner knows. A sticky half-screen shows one real product view (receipt, KOT slip, GST summary, day report) that swaps as the column beside it scrolls through order to KOT to bill to GST return.
**Hero:** the stage on one side, headline and demo button on the other.
**Live thing:** scroll builds the bill, and it holds a real receipt.
**Risk:** receipts exist in every POS, so the idea is category-wide and does not use the name. It is calmer and more informative, and easier to build and to keep accurate.

## Recommendation
Direction A. It is the only idea here a competitor could not copy, and it ties the orange to the name. If you want a calmer, more explanatory page, choose B.
